OUR STORY

A Journey Inspired by Odia Tradition

Odisha Rasoi was created with one simple idea — to bring the authentic taste of traditional Odia food to the people of Bhubaneswar.

Odisha has a rich culinary heritage shaped by generations of home cooking, local ingredients, temple traditions, and regional flavors.

Our kitchen takes inspiration from these traditions while presenting them in a warm, welcoming restaurant experience.

Every dish is prepared with carefully selected ingredients, traditional spices, and recipes inspired by Odisha's diverse food culture.
